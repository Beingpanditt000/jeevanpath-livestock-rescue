🛡️ LIVESTOCK DISASTER RESCUE & EVACUATION NETWORK

Rapid Emergency Coordination for Rural Cattle & Buffalo

During annual monsoon floods and sudden embankment breaches, rural livestock owners struggle to evacuate heavy cattle. JEEVANPATH unites Farmers, Volunteer Responders, and Rescue Teams to coordinate emergency transport, allocate safe shelter capacity, and prevent livestock mortality.
